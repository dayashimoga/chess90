## 1. Pedagogical Standards & Quality Rubric
Every day was forensically audited against the 11 strict pedagogical criteria:
1. **Concrete Explanation & Strategic Role**: No generic filler; deep breakdown of tactical/strategic mechanics.
2. **Candidate Move Generation**: Rigorous CCT (Checks, Captures, Threats) candidate identification.
3. **Refutation Analysis**: Explicit explanation of why tempting wrong candidate choices fail.
4. **Valid FEN & Legal Solutions**: 100% legal moves verified by `chess_core` MoveGenerator.
5. **Progressive Hints**: Structured hints guiding the learner without immediately revealing solutions.
6. **Solution Explanations**: Rich conceptual rationale for every move.
7. **Master Worked Models**: 2 curated model examples per day.
8. **Historic Game Study**: Linked classic or modern master games for deep contextual study.
9. **Practice Assignment**: Dedicated sparring tasks with target centipawn blunder thresholds.
10. **Remediation Protocol**: Clear recovery drills and Leitner SRS flashcards for failed attempts.
11. **Educational Integrity**: Day 90 contains official FIDE title and rating disclaimers.
