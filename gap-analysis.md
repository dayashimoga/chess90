**System:** ChessMaster 90-Day Grandmaster Chess Learning & Training Platform  
**Audit Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Auditor Roles:** Principal Chess-Software Architect, Chess Curriculum Engineer, Flutter UX Lead, QA/Automation Lead, DevSecOps/SRE  
**Standard:** Clean-room runtime reproduction, zero unverified PASS claims, zero placeholder content.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Definition of DONE

An exhaustive forensic audit of the `ChessMaster` codebase revealed that while existing test suites claimed "100% PASS" and "Production Ready", critical runtime failure modes severely undermined the core educational promise.

### Definition of DONE
A feature or curriculum component is considered **DONE** only when:
$$\text{DONE} = \text{IMPLEMENTED} \land \text{CORRECT} \land \text{INTEGRATED} \land \text{VISIBLE} \land \text{INTERACTIVE} \land \text{TESTED} \land \text{DOCUMENTED} \land \text{RUNTIME-PROVEN}$$

No placeholder, mock, dummy, or hardcoded PASS is permitted.

## 3. Remediation Architecture & Engineering Strategy

The remediation is executed systematically across five core layers:
1. **Core Content Compiler (`tool/content_compiler.dart`):** Validates all 90 curriculum days, training banks, and model games. Guarantees 100% legal FENs, valid SAN solutions, true checks/mates, non-duplication, and concept alignment.
2. **Pedagogy & Teaching Engine (`packages/chess_labs/lib/src/board_teaching_engine.dart`):** Implements Socratic interaction primitives (highlight, rays, arrows, ghost pieces, move refutations, candidate comparisons).
3. **Real Playable Mini-Games (`packages/chess_labs/lib/src/labs/playable_mini_games.dart`):** 12 interactive multi-round chess drills with legal engine/opponent responses.
4. **Responsive Chess Workspace (`ResponsiveChessWorkspace`):** True available dimension fitting, disabled buttons at true boundaries, AUTO, MAX, FULLSCREEN modes.
5. **Continuous Context & Seamless Workflow Routing:** Unified `GameSession` propagation from Play to Analysis, Train Mistake, and Video Studio.
