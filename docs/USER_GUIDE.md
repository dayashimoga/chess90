# ChessMaster User Guide

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **ChessMaster**, an offline-first, cross-platform chess mastery system designed to build deep Grandmaster-style calculation, strategic vision, endgame precision, and disciplined tournament habits over a structured 90-day program.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Product Principle & Disclaimer**:
> ChessMaster teaches the cognitive methodologies, calculation discipline, and strategic principles utilized by Grandmasters. Completing this 90-day program will dramatically elevate your chess understanding, tactical accuracy, and rating. However, ChessMaster **never promises or awards an official FIDE Grandmaster (GM) title**, which requires official FIDE-rated tournament norms and a FIDE rating of 2500+.

---

## 2. The Core Mastery Loop
Every training day in ChessMaster follows the active learning cycle:
$$\text{Diagnostic} \rightarrow \text{Skill Graph} \rightarrow \text{Adaptive Daily Plan} \rightarrow \text{Labs} \rightarrow \text{Serious Game} \rightarrow \text{Self-Analysis} \rightarrow \text{Engine Analysis} \rightarrow \text{Root Cause} \rightarrow \text{Retraining} \rightarrow \text{Exam}$$

No reading-only lectures or passive video watching are permitted; every concept must be proven on the interactive board.

---

## 3. Navigation & Main Features

### 3.1 Daily Journey Screen
- **Today's Mission**: Displays your customized schedule (Tactics, Calculation, Strategy, Endgame, Model Game, Serious Game, Spaced Review).
- **Time Modes**: Choose between **8h Intensive GM**, **60m Standard**, or **15m Express** daily modes.
- **Skill Radar**: An interactive 12-axis radar polygon comparing your current mastery level against your Day 1 baseline.

### 3.2 90-Day Curriculum
- Browse all 10 pedagogical phases across all 90 days.
- Track daily completion, test scores, and upcoming mastery gates.
- Take weekly exams on days **7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63, 70, 77, 84, 90**.

### 3.3 Interactive Labs
Practice specific cognitive skills in 16 specialized sandboxes:
- **Tactical Lab**: Find forcing shots, solve combinations, or declare *"No Tactic Exists"* in anti-puzzles.
- **Candidate Selection Lab**: Identify top candidate moves before calculating deeper lines using the Kotov method.
- **Blind Calculation Lab**: Calculate sequences mentally from a starting position, verifying terminal position without interim visual updates.
- **Endgame Win / Defend Lab**: Master Lucena, Philidor, and Queen vs 7th pawn against an active engine opponent.
- **Board Memory & Visualization Labs**: Rapid coordinate literacy and unseen diagonal calculation.
- **Find the Plan & Positional Evaluation Labs**: Strategic planning in closed positions and static evaluation scoring.
- **Improve Worst Piece & Pawn Break Labs**: Finding inactive pieces and calculating central tension breakthrough timing.
- **Pawn Structure & Opening Plan Labs**: Carlsbad, IQP, Maróczy structure recognition and opening-to-middlegame transitions.
- **Guess-The-Move, Defensive Resourcefulness, Conversion & Time Management Labs**: Simulating historic GM games, finding swindles, clean conversion, and blitz decision making under clock stress.
- *Hint Penalty*: Each progressive hint deducts 20% from your lab score; wrong moves deduct 25%.

### 3.4 Play & Serious Tournament Mode
- Play against the embedded engine, native Stockfish, or a human opponent locally.
- Choose standard tournament time controls: **45+15**, **60+30**, or **90+30 classical**.
- **Tournament Mode**: In tournament mode, all engine assistance, evaluation bars, and takebacks are disabled. Clocks decrement strictly, simulating real tournament conditions.

### 3.5 Game Analysis & Root-Cause Diagnosis
When your game concludes, navigate to the Analysis Workspace:
1. **Self-Analysis**: Replay the game manually and type your original thought process and candidate moves into the commentary box.
2. **Engine Audit**: Request engine evaluation to detect inaccuracies, mistakes, and blunders.
3. **Root-Cause Diagnosis**: The engine automatically classifies your errors into 11 root causes (e.g., impulsive move, time trouble, tactical blind spot, pawn structure error).
4. **Spaced Retraining**: Classified blunders are automatically queued into your Leitner spaced repetition flashcard box.

### 3.6 Model Games & Guess-The-Move
- Study public-domain master games (Paul Morphy, Jose Raul Capablanca, Bobby Fischer, Akiba Rubinstein).
- Play in **Guess-The-Move** mode: The board pauses at each move; you must calculate and find the master's choice.

### 3.7 Video Studio
- Select any played game or study.
- Choose your video format: **YouTube 16:9**, **Shorts / Reels 9:16**, **Social Square 1:1**, or **Animated GIF**.
- Scrub through the visual timeline with evaluation bars and tactical arrows.
- Click **Export Script** or **Generate Video** to render high-definition videos via FFmpeg.

### 3.8 Day 90 Certification
- Upon completing Day 90 and passing the final comprehensive exam, view your **Day 1 vs. Day 90 Mastery Report**.
- Reviews your skill growth across all 12 axes.
- Generates your personalized Roadmap for the next 90 days of competitive play.
