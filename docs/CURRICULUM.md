# 90-Day GM-Style Curriculum Syllabus

The ChessMaster 90-day program is organized into 10 vertical phases with 13 mandatory weekly exam gates:

## Phase 1: Baseline Diagnostic & Personalized Skill Graph (Day 1)
- **Day 1**: Comprehensive diagnostic battery assessing coordinates, board vision, forks, pins, calculation depth, candidate move generation, static evaluation, King+Pawn opposition, opening principles, clock pacing, and serious game baseline.

## Phase 2: Tactical Foundation & Pattern Vision (Days 2–14)
- **Day 2**: Attacked, Defended & Hanging Pieces (LPDO principle)
- **Day 3**: Checks, Captures & Threats (Forcing move discipline)
- **Day 4**: Knight Forks & Double Attacks
- **Day 5**: Absolute & Relative Pins, Skewers
- **Day 6**: Discovered Attacks & Discovered Checks
- **Day 7**: **WEEKLY EXAM 1** (Tactics Gate 1 - Threshold: >=85%)
- **Day 8**: Decoy & Deflection
- **Day 9**: Attraction & Interference
- **Day 10**: Clearance & Overload of Defender
- **Day 11**: Trapped Pieces, Zwischenzug & Desperado
- **Day 12**: X-Ray & Promotion Tactics
- **Day 13**: Back-Rank Motifs, Mating Nets & "No Tactic Exists" Recognition
- **Day 14**: **WEEKLY EXAM 2** (Tactics Foundation Mastery Gate - Threshold: >=85%)

## Phase 3: Concrete Calculation & Visualization Trees (Days 15–28)
- **Day 15**: Candidate Generation & Checks/Captures/Threats (Kotov's Tree)
- **Day 16**: Quiet Moves & Prophylactic Defense
- **Day 17**: Opponent's Best Reply vs Wishful Thinking
- **Day 18**: Move Ordering & Branch Management
- **Day 19**: Branch Pruning & Depth Management
- **Day 20**: Stopping Points & Static Evaluation at Horizon
- **Day 21**: **WEEKLY EXAM 3** (Calculation Gate 1)
- **Day 22**: Blindfold Calculation & Mental Stepping
- **Day 23**: Board Geometry Recall & Memory Drills
- **Day 24**: Defensive Counter-Punches in Deep Calculation
- **Day 25**: Anti-Puzzles: When Intuitive Sacrifices Fail
- **Day 26**: Calculation Under Complex Tension
- **Day 27**: Intermediate Moves in Deep Trees
- **Day 28**: **WEEKLY EXAM 4** (Calculation & Visualization Mastery Gate)

## Phase 4: Positional Strategy & Pawn Structures (Days 29–42)
- **Day 29**: Material Imbalance & Compensation
- **Day 30**: King Safety & Pawn Shelter
- **Day 31**: Development, Initiative & Tempo
- **Day 32**: Space Advantage & Pawn Centers
- **Day 33**: Weak Squares & Permanent Outposts
- **Day 34**: Open & Semi-Open Files, 7th Rank Infiltration
- **Day 35**: **WEEKLY EXAM 5** (Positional Strategy Gate 1)
- **Day 36**: Diagonals, Bishop Pair & Good vs Bad Bishops
- **Day 37**: Knight vs Bishop Dynamics

- **Day 42**: **WEEKLY EXAM 6** (Strategy & Pawn Structure Mastery Gate)

## Phase 5: Theoretical Endgames & Engine Conversion (Days 43–56)
- **Day 43**: Opposition, Key Squares & Triangulation
- **Day 44**: Corresponding Squares & Zugzwang
- **Day 45**: Pawn Breakthroughs & Outside Passed Pawns
